CRM with AI Data Collection

"Stop entering data. Start using it."

Most CRMs are glorified spreadsheets. Sales teams spend hours manually entering contact details, logging calls, and updating deal stages. Meanwhile, leads go cold and data gets stale.

Our AI-powered CRM layer captures data automatically from every source — web forms, social media, email, phone calls, and messaging apps. It enriches customer profiles, deduplicates records, and builds a clean, real-time picture of every lead and customer without anyone lifting a finger.

It layers on top of what you already use — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Bitrix24, or whatever your team relies on.

What you get:

  • Automatic lead capture from all channels
  • AI-enriched customer profiles (company info, social data, intent signals)
  • Smart lead scoring based on real engagement
  • Seamless sync with your existing CRM
  • Real-time dashboards showing pipeline health

Built for: Sales teams drowning in data entry. Operations managers who need clean reporting. Growing businesses whose CRM is becoming a chore instead of a tool.

Pricing: Depends on your pipeline size, number of data sources, and CRM in use. We scope it after a quick audit.

AI Quality Control for Phone Calls

"You can't coach what you can't hear."

Your sales and support teams make dozens of calls every day. Some go well. Some don't. But without listening to every call — and knowing what to look for — you're coaching based on gut feeling, not data.

Our AI system records, transcribes, and analyses every sales and support call. It scores each conversation against your custom criteria — compliance, objection handling, script adherence, tone, and talk-to-listen ratio. Managers get real-time dashboards, coaching queues with specific timestamps, and compliance alerts before they become problems.

What you get:

  • Automatic recording, transcription, and analysis of every call
  • Customisable scoring criteria built around your standards
  • Performance dashboards for individuals and teams
  • Compliance breach flagging in real time
  • Coaching queues with specific moments flagged for review
  • Trend reporting — are your teams improving over time?

Built for: Sales directors who want to scale coaching. Operations managers with compliance requirements. Businesses where phone conversations are the difference between closing and losing.

Pricing: Based on call volume, team size, and analytics depth.
